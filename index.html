Know what the numbers mean

i
CT
Completion time

When the process finishes.

CT = finish time
TAT
Turnaround time

Total time from arrival to finish.

TAT = CT − AT
WT
Waiting time

Time spent waiting in the ready queue.

WT = TAT − BT
RT
Response time

Time until the process first gets the CPU.

RT = first start − AT
01

Non-preemptive
A running process keeps the CPU until its burst finishes.

02

Preemptive
A newly arrived higher-priority process can interrupt the running process.

03

Watch out for starvation
Low-priority processes may wait a long time if higher-priority work keeps arriving.
